## Seasonal Launcher Icon System
Status: **FINAL**

The approved launcher artwork remains independent of the product-name status. The no-shield landscape composition is locked unless the FINAL decision is explicitly reopened.

### Geometry
- No shield border/container.
- Same core artwork across all themes.
- Fill Android launcher masks: circle, square, rounded square.
- Keep composition visually centered across adaptive-icon masks.

### Preserve exactly as closely as practical
Do not:
- add/remove trees,
- change mountain silhouettes,
- change the river/path shape,
- move the sun,
- restyle the artwork between themes.

Only scaling/cropping for masks and palette recolouring are permitted.

### Seasonal palettes
- **Spring:** fresher, lighter, less green-heavy.
- **Summer:** approved current green/gold palette.
- **Autumn:** green retained with restrained autumn orange/gold.
- **Winter:** approved current cool blue/cream palette.
