# TrailCharter Vision

TrailCharter is a privacy-first, offline-first, open-source Android expedition and adventure companion.

It is not simply another mapping application. It should help users plan, organise, undertake and remember complete adventures.

The **current product/release geography is the United Kingdom only**: England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland. This deliberately narrows mapping, offline-package, testing and product complexity while the application is established. Crown Dependencies and other countries are outside the current scope and may be reconsidered later.

TrailCharter is designed as a device-resident application rather than a client for a TrailCharter cloud platform. Core data and computation remain on the user's device, and core functionality must not depend on servers owned or administered by the project.

OpenStreetMap is the intended mapping foundation. Organic Maps may be reused or integrated where appropriate, but TrailCharter must retain its own identity.

Current product territory includes driving, parking, walking stages, bothies, campsites, accommodation, multiple hikes from a base, return journeys, itineraries, GPX, equipment, food/water, pack weight, notes, journals and safety-oriented planning within the UK.

Live location sharing is outside the current product/release scope. Location tracking remains local to the device unless that decision is deliberately reopened in a future scope review.

Conceptual model: **Prepare → Adventure → Remember**.

Product name status: **TrailCharter — AGREE, not yet FINAL**.
