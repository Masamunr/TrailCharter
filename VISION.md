# Altrove Vision

Altrove is a privacy-first, offline-first, open-source Android expedition and adventure companion.

It is not simply another mapping application. It should help users plan, organise, undertake and remember complete adventures.

OpenStreetMap is the intended mapping foundation. Organic Maps may be reused or integrated where appropriate, but Altrove must retain its own identity.

Long-term product territory includes driving, parking, walking stages, bothies, campsites, accommodation, multiple hikes from a base, return journeys, itineraries, GPX, equipment, food/water, pack weight, notes, journals and safety-oriented planning.

Conceptual model: **Prepare → Adventure → Remember**.
